About Jyoti Medical Agencies

Your Trusted Partner in IV Fluid Supply

Jyoti Medical Agencies (JMA) is a wholesale distributor of IV fluids and essential medical supplies based in Akkayapalem, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. We are committed to serving hospitals, clinics, nursing homes, pharmacies, and other healthcare institutions with reliable product supply and professional service.

We supply a wide range of IV fluids, including Normal Saline (NS), Ringer Lactate (RL), DNS, D5, TNA Peri, TNA Central, Sterile Water, and other essential IV solutions from reputed healthcare brands such as Puniska Healthcare, IHL Lifesciences, Aculife, and Fresenius Kabi.

"At Jyoti Medical Agencies, we focus on product quality, dependable supply, timely service, and long-term customer relationships. Our goal is to support healthcare providers by helping them meet their medical supply requirements efficiently."

Whether you operate a multi-specialty hospital, an emergency trauma center, a community nursing home, or a high-volume retail pharmacy, JMA provides consistent inventory, authentic batch certification, and rapid doorstep delivery across Visakhapatnam and surrounding coastal Andhra regions.
